✳ GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

We want you to feel informed and confident before your next visit.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Many patients benefit from routine checkups around every six months. Your dentist may recommend a different schedule based on your needs.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Contact your dental provider promptly for severe pain, swelling, injury or a broken tooth. Seek urgent medical attention for breathing or swallowing difficulties.

Do you provide dental care for children?

Yes, our family-oriented approach includes gentle dental care for children of different ages.

What are the options for missing teeth?

Depending on your situation, options may include implants, bridges or dentures. A consultation can help determine what is appropriate.

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

A dentist can evaluate your oral health and recommend an appropriate whitening option.
